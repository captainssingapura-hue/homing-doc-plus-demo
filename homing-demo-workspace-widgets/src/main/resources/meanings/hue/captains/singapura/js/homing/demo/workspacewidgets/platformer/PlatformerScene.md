# The platformer's scene

## PlatformerScene

What the platformer's stage shows around the animal: where it plays, what it lands on, what it must not fall into, and how a run ends.

## Backdrop

The ground of the stage itself, behind everything in it.

## Sky

The band of sky along the top of the stage.

## Platform

A ledge the animal can land on and jump from.

## PlatformUnderfoot

The ledge the animal is standing on right now.

## Lava

What waits at the foot of the stage: the run ends when the animal falls into it.

## ScoreCard

The card over the stage once a run is over, with the score and the way to play again.
