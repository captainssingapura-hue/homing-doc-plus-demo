# A small taxonomy with parts, over the house's roles

## Text

Words the reader takes in.

## Container

Holds others as one thing to the user.

## Pane

Holds work in a frame of its own.

## Heading

Says what a container holds.

## Caption

Quiet words beside a thing.

## FloatingPane

Holds work over the page, moved by the user.

## Card

Stands for one thing among others.

## Dialog

Puts one matter to the user above the page.
